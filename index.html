    FAQ & Skin Care Resources

    How do I Know my skin type

    Oily skin benefits from a gentle gel cleanser, a lightweight oil-free moisturizer, niacinamide serum, and a matte sunscreen. Avoid heavy creams.

    How long does it take to see results?

    Visible results usually appear within 3–4 weeks of consistent skincare, depending on your skin type and routine.

    How often should I follow my skincare routine?

    A basic skincare routine should be followed twice daily — once in the morning and once at night — for best results.

    Is sunscreen necessary for all skin types?

    Yes, sunscreen is essential for all skin types. It protects the skin from sun damage, premature aging, and pigmentation.

    Can I use home remedies along with skincare products?

    Yes, natural home remedies like aloe vera, honey, and rose water can be used alongside products. Always do a patch test before applying.
